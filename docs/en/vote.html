HOW TO VOTE

1

Download the full Mulacoin package for your operating system.

Get it at mulacoin.com.br/en

2

Extract the zip and run the startup script:

🐧 Linux: ./iniciar-mulacoin.sh

🪟 Windows: Iniciar-Mulacoin.bat

The wallet and voting interface open automatically.

3

Wait for the wallet to sync with the Mulacoin network. Then select a topic, choose your option and click "Register Vote on Blockchain".

4

Track results in real time on this page. Anyone can independently audit all votes directly on the blockchain.

🔍 PROTOCOL TRANSPARENCY

All vote transactions are permanently recorded on the Mulacoin blockchain — including duplicate vote attempts. Anyone can audit the complete history. The protocol rule is simple and immutable:

MVOTE:1:VOTE:TOPIC_ID:option
                ↑
  First record per address = VALID
  Subsequent records      = IGNORED
Feature Traditional Election Mulacoin Vote
Vote confirmation❌ Can't verify your vote was counted✅ TX confirmed = recorded forever
Audit access❌ Restricted to authorized experts✅ Anyone can audit
Result integrity❌ Can be contested✅ Blockchain is immutable
Duplicate votes❌ Hidden fraud✅ Visible and automatically ignored
Transparency❌ Closed process✅ 100% public
Historical record❌ Data can be altered✅ Immutable forever
⚠️ Known limitation: The Mulacoin Vote protocol operates on the principle of 1 address = 1 vote, not 1 person = 1 vote. A person with multiple funded addresses can technically vote more than once. This is an inherent limitation shared by other blockchain governance projects like Dogecoin and Bitcoin. All votes — including potential duplicates — are permanently visible on the blockchain for public audit. Mulacoin Vote is an experimental decentralized democracy system and does not replace official electoral systems.

💡 How to audit: Anyone with the Mulacoin wallet and txindex=1 enabled can scan the full blockchain and independently verify all votes. The MVOTE protocol ensures only the first vote per address is counted — making fraud mathematically impossible to hide.
